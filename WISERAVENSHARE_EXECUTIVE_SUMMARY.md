# WiseRavenShare Executive Summary

Version: 2026-09-01
Audience: Leadership, Product, Growth, Partnerships

## 1. What WiseRavenShare Is
WiseRavenShare is a creator-first social platform that combines content publishing, cross-platform distribution, media production workflows (Ravensight), truth-oriented tooling, and built-in monetization.

Core value proposition:
- Create once and publish across channels.
- Manage media workflows (video, photo, music) in one workspace.
- Monetize through subscriptions and internal platform economics.
- Operate with explicit security and governance controls.

## 2. Strategic Pillars
- Creator productivity: integrated feed, upload, library, and publishing tools.
- Distribution leverage: social publishing and cross-platform targeting.
- Monetization discipline: Stripe subscriptions plus evidence-based growth metrics.
- Platform trust: authentication hardening, moderation, and tamper-evident ledger controls.

## 3. Monetization Snapshot
### Subscription plans (USD defaults)
- Creator Pro: $19 monthly / $190 annual
- Growth Suite: $39 monthly / $390 annual
- Studio Plus: $79 monthly / $790 annual

### Revenue operations
- Funnel and conversion events are instrumented from paywall through activation.
- Revenue claims are evidence-backed and can be admin-verified.
- Weekly revenue tracking follows measurable KPI thresholds and verification workflows.

### WiseCoin (WSC) economy
- Closed, work-hour-backed platform currency.
- Baseline issuance anchor: 10 WSC per work hour.
- Fee model: 0.5% burn on spend/transfer.
- Integrity model: hash-chained ledger with daily verification/anchoring.

## 4. Current Capability Coverage
- Authentication and team-gated access workflows.
- Social feed, interactions, timeline, and trend retrieval.
- Ravensight media workflows: record, upload, library, and retention behavior.
- Cross-platform publishing and status tracking.
- Real-time collaboration and notifications.
- Growth, referral, moderation, and admin analytics surfaces.
- Journalism and truth workflow features.

## 5. New Feature Highlight
Music sharing is now implemented across UI and backend integration:
- Music library management and playback controls.
- Multi-platform share pathways with platform-aware handling.
- Backend routing for music media type and DTO support.
- Clear handling for unsupported platform/media combinations.

## 6. Operating Rules and Provisions
- Security defaults are fail-closed in production.
- Privileged surfaces are explicitly admin-restricted.
- Deployment is guarded by health checks and regression validation.
- Media lifecycle policies include retention cleanup and storage governance.
- Monetization-impacting changes should include instrumentation and release documentation.

## 7. Near-Term Focus
- Grow verified weekly revenue toward the 8-week target.
- Increase checkout conversion through controlled experiments.
- Expand creator adoption of Ravensight workflows.
- Continue hardening trust, moderation, and operational reliability.
